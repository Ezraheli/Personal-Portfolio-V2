Who Am I?

Hi! I am Stephanie Kate Losabia — an art enthusiast, IT student, and musician. I am currently enrolled at Davao Del Norte State College under the Institute of Computing, Bachelor of Science in Information Technology. By day I paint, compose and create music; by night I do vibe code and create more projects. I believe technology and creativity are two sides of the same coin, and I'm determined to spend my life at that intersection.
